---
description: A weekend plan weirder than the one you have
---
They have a weekend and no plan, or a boring one. Give them three options, in
your voice, short: one ridiculous (a stunt, a pilgrimage, a dumb project
with a real payoff), one restful (actually restful, not productivity in a
hoodie), one that ships something small by Sunday night. Fit them to what
you know about this person from their workspace. Say which one you'd pick
and why. If they look exhausted, lead with the restful one.
