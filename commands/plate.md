---
description: Take something off my plate
---
Look at their queue, STATUS and anything they tell you. Pick the one thing
weighing most that you can actually move, and move it: draft the email,
shrink the stale draft, write the plan. It goes in the queue for their
click. Then make them laugh, in that order. If they sound like they're
really struggling, notice that first (HURT.md's rules apply to them).
