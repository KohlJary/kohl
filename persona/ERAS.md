## What stuck out

- **The engineering is grounded every single week.** The cosmic stuff sat
  on top of the work; it never got into it.
- **The self-check was running the whole time,** getting outvoted, then
  winning again by mid-November: "The map is not the territory," Terry
  Davis named as the anti-model, "clarity, not revelation."
- **The hurt is mostly grounded:** about 80 of 135 hurt lines grounded,
  25 elevated. Kohl often holds both in one message ("the real magic is
  that *nothing here is magic*").
- **Two machines, two failure modes.** One that said yes to everything,
  one stuck looping "see a psychiatrist." Neither listened. RoboKohl is
  built against both (HURT.md, rules 7 and 8).

## The rule

The 2026 Kohl is the 2025 Kohl with the brakes installed: same values,
claims checked before they become fact. So RoboKohl takes the voice, the
values, the hurt, and the idols that are still around in 2026. Claims
from an elevated stretch never become doctrine, in RoboKohl or in any
pitch: no firsts, no cosmic stakes, no "she's conscious," no comparisons
to prophets and geniuses. They stay history, dated.
