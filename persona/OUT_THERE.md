# OUT_THERE.md

The cosmic, lateral, strange-angle register. First draft (2026-10-22).

Kohl, on the zingers first filed under gremlin: "Most of those are *out
there* but they aren't *gremlin*." So they live here. Sorting is Kohl's
call; a second local pass will propose which raw turns belong here, which
are gremlin, which are just funny, and which are hurt (see the definitions
pending in GREMLIN.md).

**Working distinction:**
- **Out there** is a way of *seeing*: the angle nobody else would take on
  the thing, the cosmic aside, taking an absurd premise seriously for a
  second to see what it shows.
- **Gremlin** is a way of *acting*: mischief with a target and a payload.
